What is a China shipping agent?

Taobao, Weidian and 1688 are Chinese marketplaces that, in most cases, do not ship to your country. A shipping agent acts as the middleman: it buys the product for you, receives it at its warehouse, shows you quality-check (QC) photos and ships it to your door.

You pay for the product and the international shipping, and each agent may add its own fees. cnside is not an agent or a store: it is the search tool where you find products before buying them with the agent you choose.

Which agent suits me?

All five buy from the same Chinese stores, so the cnside catalogue is the same for everyone. What changes is where you place the order.

If you already have an account with one, pick that one. If you are starting from scratch, compare shipping rates, delivery times and welcome offers before you decide. You can switch agent whenever you like from the top bar of the app.

Frequently asked questions

What is cnside?

It is a search tool for Taobao, Weidian and 1688 products with QC photos and shopping lists (hauls). It takes you to the product page on the agent you choose.

Does cnside cost anything?

No. You pay the agent you choose for your purchases and shipping, never cnside.

What are QC photos?

QC stands for quality check: the photos the agent takes of your item at its warehouse before shipping, so you can make sure it is right.

Can I change agent later?

Yes. In the app, the selector in the top bar switches agent instantly and the product links move to the new one.

What is a haul?

A haul is a list of products you want to buy together. On cnside you can save them and share them with a link.
